## The key message in one sentence

> ARGUS records the system's history in a way that **cannot be rewritten silently** — and if
> anyone tries, the record itself exposes the attempt.

---

## Three selling points

| Selling point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Hash chain + independent certificate** | An admissible record, not just a log file |
| **Self-protection (kernel shield)** | An attacker cannot turn recording off |
| **Never requires a reboot** | Deployable in production without taking services down |
